# Roadmap

## Phase 1 — Foundation (v0.1, done)
Local-first app with plants, zones, logs, feed schedules, calculator, inventory, lights, soil, yield, history, backup.

## Phase 2 — Daily use
- Installable PWA, works offline on phone
- Photos on log entries
- Week-based schedules that advance automatically; feed/feed/water rotation
- pH/EC out-of-range warnings; PPM ↔ EC
- Shopping list from low stock
- Temperature / humidity log and VPD

## Phase 3 — Garden planning and soil
- Frost-date planting calendar
- Crop rotation history per bed
- Soil test trends and amendment suggestions
- Compost tracking

## Phase 4 — Connected
- Backend (see ARCHITECTURE.md) and multi-device sync
- Reminders by push or email
- Sensor ingestion (Home Assistant / MQTT / pH-EC pens)
- Cost per harvest, yearly report, run comparisons
